We didn’t set out to invent anything. We didn’t know where to start. We only knew we weren’t satisfied with the status quo. That search took us across the U.S., through Europe, and eventually into India, looking for answers and, ultimately, for the roots of yoga. In one of the oldest ashrams, we watched yogis rinse their nasal passages every morning at 4 a.m. without instruction or explanation. It was simply what they did. This is where MACKLEY begins.

The Original Neti Pot™ is a simple tool for clearing the nasal passage. It has been used for thousands of years, and its design has endured because it works. We didn’t invent anything new or add features. We sought out the best materials, worked with the best manufacturers, and preserved the time-tested form so the flush works the way it always has.

Warm saline water enters one nostril, flows through the nasal cavity, and exits the other, carrying out mucus, dust, pollen, and airborne particles that accumulate through normal breathing. This flow reaches deeper than blowing the nose, including the upper nasal cavity behind the eyes. When the nasal passage is clear, air moves into the lungs with less resistance. Breathing depth often increases automatically, without effort or instruction.

Water and salt are essential to this process. Saline solution supports the mucosal lining, which is part of the nose’s filtration system. Proper salinity reduces irritation and inflammation so nasal tissue can function normally. Warm water improves comfort and consistency. Gravity moves the water through the passage. The correct tilt ensures the flow is strong enough to be effective, without pressure.

The material of the pot matters because the nasal cavity sits close to the blood–brain barrier. Plastic degrades over time and sheds microscopic particles. Ceramic is inert but fragile and slippery when wet. Copper is naturally antimicrobial, durable, and has been used for water vessels for centuries. A properly finished copper surface resists bacterial growth between uses. This pot is made from 99.99% pure copper.

There is no handle. Without one, you naturally lift your elbow higher, which creates the correct angle for gravity to flush the cavity. The spout is angled at 40° to seal comfortably against the nostril and guide the flow. The body volume is sized so one fill typically clears both sides. At roughly 200 grams, the weight provides stability in the hand.

Each pot is made using traditional methods. Pure copper plates are heated, cooled, and shaped on a lathe until the body is smooth and even. There are no seams or sharp edges. The spout is formed separately and welded with bronze. The pot is acid-washed to remove impurities, polished, finished with a food-grade coating, inspected, and shipped.

The goal is a tool with a long lever of improvement in daily life. Used regularly, nasal rinsing works as maintenance rather than repair. Most people keep the pot in the shower or near their toothbrush. Clear airways support deeper breathing. Everything downstream depends on that.
