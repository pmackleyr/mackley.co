Questions

What is jala neti?

Jala neti is the yogic practice of rinsing your nose with salt water. A neti pot uses gravity to wash away dust, pollen and mucus.

How do I use it?

Fill the clean pot with lukewarm saline. Lean over a sink, tilt your head sideways and breathe through your mouth. Pour gently into the upper nostril so water flows out the lower one. Repeat on the other side. Ask a health professional how often to rinse for your needs.

What water and salt should I use?

Use distilled or sterile water, or tap water that has been boiled and cooled. Never use water straight from the tap. Use a nasal saline mix and follow its water measurements. Read the CDC guidance.

Is nasal rinsing right for everyone?

Do not rinse if your nose is fully blocked. Ask a health professional first if your immune system is weak, or before using it with a child. Stop and get medical advice if you have pain, nosebleeds, fever or headaches.

Why copper?

Copper is durable and has long been used for jala neti. This pot is hand-shaped and made for daily use. Copper does not replace washing, safe water or proper drying.

How do I clean and dry it?

Wash with mild soap and safe water after each use. Rinse well. Dry the outside with the towel. Leave the pot upside down until the inside is fully dry. Copper can darken over time.
