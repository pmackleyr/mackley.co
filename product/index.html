Now read on to understand why we built this:

Semax and Selank were developed by Russian scientists during the 1970s and 1980s as part of a broader effort to understand how the brain learns, adapts, and performs under stress. Semax originated from research on a naturally occurring hormone called ACTH, while Selank was derived from tuftsin, a naturally occurring peptide involved in immune function. In both cases, researchers modified these molecules to preserve their benefits while improving stability and minimizing unwanted side effects.

Semax was initially designed to support recovery after stroke and brain injury, but researchers quickly observed broader effects on attention, memory, learning, and cognition. Rather than forcing the brain into a heightened state like a stimulant, Semax is intended to support neuroplasticity so you can more easily learn, adapt, and grow through new experiences.

Selank is complementary. It was originally developed to help reduce anxiety and improve emotional resilience, but researchers also noticed effects on calmness, focus, and clarity. Unlike traditional anti-anxiety medications, Selank is intended to help the nervous system reduce noise and focus on what matters most.

Oxytocin, the third ingredient in the INF™ formula, was not developed in a lab because it is a naturally occurring hormone and neuropeptide that helps form the backbone of human connection. Oxytocin was first identified and isolated in the early 1900s while studying childbirth and breastfeeding, where it plays a central role in uterine contractions and milk letdown. Over time, researchers discovered that oxytocin's influence extends far beyond reproduction. It is released during moments of trust, bonding, affection, and connection. It helps shape how humans relate to one another, from the bond between mother and child to friendships, romantic relationships, teamwork, and social cooperation. Because of this, oxytocin became known as the "love hormone."

Learning and growth are not purely cognitive. Human beings learn through imitation, trust, belonging, and community. And oxytocin appears to underlie many of these experiences. Semax can help your brain adapt. Selank can help reduce noise. Oxytocin can help strengthen connection in the very environments where we learn and grow.

The goal of this formula is neither stimulation nor sedation. INF™ exists to encourage a new state primed for learning, action, and growth. Not so you can take another product and wait for your life to change, but so you can enter the moments that change you with more clarity, calm, and connection. It is the combination of these three neuropeptides that makes INF™ a category of its own.
