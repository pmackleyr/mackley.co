Neuroplasticity is your brain's ability to change.

Like a muscle, what you repeatedly think, feel, and do shapes your brain over time.

At first, change can be difficult. But as you repeatedly take steps in the right direction, progress becomes more automatic. Growth becomes habit. And before you realise it, you're a better version of yourself.

State → Action → Growth

Your "State" is where your mind is before you decide to change.

Practices like breathing, meditation, and prayer can help calm your mind, reduce noise, and harmonise thought, emotion, and action. But not all states are created equal.

Other approaches, like caffeine and antidepressants, influence mental state by increasing stimulation or suppressing symptoms. And while this may be valuable in certain circumstances, they're often not designed with the long-term You in mind.

INF™ was designed around the idea that meaningful change begins with the right state. One where you're calm in your body, clearer to signal, less distracted by noise, and more present with the amazing world around you and at your very fingertips.

The formula includes three neuropeptides--Semax, Selank, and Oxytocin--each chosen for its unique role in learning, clarity, emotional resilience, and human connection.

This formula is delivered as a nasal spray because the nasal passage sits at the doorstep of the blood-brain barrier, allowing these neuropeptides to enter efficiently and your new state before action. The 30mL bottle is small enough to fit in your pocket, toiletry bag, or carry with you on the go.

The research behind these neuropeptides is actually pretty cool. Semax and Selank were developed by hardcore Russian scientists during the 1970s and 1980s who wanted to better understand how the brain learns, adapts, and performs under stress. In both cases, researchers modified naturally occurring molecules, ACTH for Semax and tuftsin for Selank, to preserve their benefits while improving stability and minimising unwanted side effects.

Semax was initially developed to support recovery after stroke and brain injury, but researchers soon observed measurable effects on attention, memory, learning, and cognition.

Rather than forcing the brain into a heightened state like a stimulant, Semax supports neuroplasticity so you can more easily learn, adapt, and grow through new experiences.

Selank is complementary to Semax. Originally developed to reduce anxiety and improve emotional resilience, researchers observed measurable effects on calmness, focus, and mental clarity.

Unlike many traditional anti-anxiety medications, Selank was designed to help the nervous system reduce noise while allowing you to remain fully present and focused on what matters most.

Oxytocin is what makes this formula truly unique. First identified and isolated in the early 1900s while studying childbirth and breastfeeding, it plays a central role in uterine contractions and milk letdown.

But over time, researchers discovered that oxytocin's influence extends far beyond reproduction.

Oxytocin is released during moments of trust, bonding, and connection. It helps shape how we relate to one another--from mother and child, to friendships, to romance, to teamwork and cooperation.

Learning and growth are not purely cognitive experiences. Human beings learn through imitation, trust, belonging, and community. Oxytocin underlies many of these core experiences.
