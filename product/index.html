INF™ exists because we believe the mind can be trained, just like your body. What you repeatedly think, feel, and do changes the structure of your brain. This ability is called neuroplasticity. The pathways you use become stronger. The pathways you ignore become weaker. Over time, your habits become your identity.

INF™ Intranasal Neuropeptide formula is designed to help you learn better, think more clearly, stay calmer under stress, and connect more deeply with the people around you. It includes three neuropeptides: Semax, Selank, and Oxytocin.

Semax was chosen to increase your neuroplasticity so you learn and evolve. It has been studied for learning, memory, attention, and cognitive performance. When you're trying to learn a new skill, build a business, master a craft, or stay focused on what matters, attention is your raw material.

Selank was chosen because learning does not happen in a vacuum. Stress, anxiety, and mental tension can interfere with attention and decision-making. Selank has been studied for its effects on calmness, emotional regulation, and mental clarity. The goal is to remain composed while staying fully engaged.

Oxytocin was chosen because human beings do not learn alone. Relationships, team, and trust matter. Oxytocin is a naturally occurring neuropeptide involved in social connection, emotional bonding, and the body's response to stress. It was included because the best tool to accompany your own clear mind, is someone else's.

Together, these three ingredients support the three fundamental functions of the human experience:

Learn.

Think.

Connect.

INF™ is delivered as an intranasal formula because the nose provides a direct route of administration across the blood-brain-barrier and into the brain, and spray-delivery is a simple daily practice that you access on-the-go.

We include the neti pot because nasal hygiene is super important. Before supporting your mind, your must clear your airway.

Each day, dust, pollen, smoke, pollution, and other particles accumulate inside the nasal cavity. A neti pot uses warm water, salt and gravity to physically flush these materials away. The result is a cleaner nasal passage and easier nasal breathing. You can mechanistically breathe deeper.

The Original Copper Neti Pot™ is hand-shaped from pure copper, a durable material that has been used by yogis for thousands of years to clear their breathe and therefore unlock their consciousness. It is designed for daily use and built to last.
