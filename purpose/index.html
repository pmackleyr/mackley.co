MACKLEY means REVOLUTION:

from who you are inside,

to what you do outside.

MACKLEY exists to close the gap between who you are inside and what you do outside, between your walk and your talk. There is greatness inside you. Call it God, Allah, Ohm, human potential. Faith is learning to breathe deeply, then act accordingly.

Whether you’ve dealt with asthma or pneumonia, a speech impediment or stutter, whether you’ve done speech & debate, boxing, jiu jitsu, surfing, or slacklining, you’ve felt it: sometimes the best thing you can do is just breathe.

Breath unites all of us. You enter this world on a breath. You leave when breathing stops. The shape of your life is, by and large, the shape of your breath.

You can go months without food, days without water, but only minutes without air. And while most people focus on diet, supplements, and brushing their teeth, almost no one clears the very passage that air moves through.

Every day, dust, pollen, and pollution build up in your nasal passage. You can’t reach it, and you can’t fully clear it by blowing your nose. It literally narrows your nasal passage, so your breathing, and life, become shallow.

At the root of yoga, the first daily practice is to clean the nose (“jala neti”). Why? Breath is life. Clear your nasal passage, and you breathe deeper. You calm down. Your mind becomes clear.

Breath is not belief. Breath is potential. When breathing is shallow, life feels rushed and noisy. When breathing is full, thought and action align. You know the difference.

Clearing your nose is the most important hygienic habit.
