Cookie Policy

This page explains what cookies and similar technologies do on MACKLEY, including analytics and Google ad measurement.

Quick summary

How cookies are used here

  • Some cookies are required for basic site behavior, checkout flow, and security.
  • Some cookies or local storage keys help us understand page views, traffic sources, and whether a visit leads to checkout or purchase intent.
  • If Google ad or conversion tags are active, Google may use cookies or similar technologies to measure impressions, clicks, and conversions.
  • You can control most cookies in your browser settings, although disabling them may affect parts of the site.

Essential

Cookies we need to operate

These technologies keep the site loading properly, preserve basic preferences, and support checkout or fraud-prevention functions when needed.

Analytics and ads

Cookies used for measurement

We may use analytics, ad tags, and conversion measurement to understand where visitors come from, which pages perform well, and which clicks lead toward checkout.

Local storage and browser storage

More than browser cookies

MACKLEY may also use local storage or similar browser storage for product preferences, checkout state, and ad or conversion debugging. We treat those tools like cookies for policy purposes.

Google technologies

When Google tools are active

Google tags or Google Ads placements may set or read identifiers to measure ad impressions, clicks, page visits, and conversion events. Google may process that information according to Google’s own privacy terms.

How to control

Your options

You can block or delete cookies through your browser, use private browsing, or use ad settings and privacy controls offered by Google and other vendors.

What happens if you disable them

Potential impact

If you block essential cookies or related browser storage, parts of the site, forms, measurement flows, or checkout handoff may not work correctly.

Questions about cookies

If you have questions about cookies, local storage, or advertising technologies on this site, email contact@mackley.co.
