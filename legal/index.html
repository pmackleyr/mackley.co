Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain English, what MACKLEY collects, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

Quick summary

What matters most

  • We collect the information needed to run the site, respond to you, fulfill orders, and measure what is working.
  • We may use forms, payment providers, analytics tools, and Google ad or conversion tools to understand traffic and purchases.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • You can ask us what data we have about you, ask us to delete it where allowed, or opt out of marketing emails at any time.

Information you give us

What we collect directly

Name, email address, shipping details, order details, and any information you type into forms, checkout, or messages you send us.

Information we collect automatically

What gets logged in the background

Basic device and browser details, pages viewed, referral URLs, ad campaign parameters, approximate timestamps, and cookie or local storage data used for site performance and conversion measurement.

How we use data

Why we collect it

We use personal information to run the site, fulfill purchases, send transactional emails, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and improve the product and site experience.

We also use traffic and conversion data to understand which pages, campaigns, and ads lead to interest or purchases.

Who receives data

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help operate MACKLEY, such as hosting, payments, email forms, analytics, fraud prevention, and advertising or conversion-measurement vendors.

Those providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to run the site, fulfill orders, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with law, and measure performance.

Your choices

What you can do

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails, block cookies in your browser, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us. Some data may still be retained when we need it for legal, tax, fraud, or order-history reasons.

Returns

Return policy

We accept returns for both defective and non-defective Original Copper Neti Pot™ orders.

Returns are accepted by mail only. Returned items must be new, unused, and in original condition. We do not accept exchanges.

The return window is lifetime. Customers are responsible for the cost of the return shipping label. We do not charge restocking fees.

To request a return, email contact@mackley.co with your order number and reason for return. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 12 days after the return is received and processed.

Children

Age guidance

This site and product are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Updates

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date shown above.

Questions or requests

Email contact@mackley.co if you want to ask about your information, request deletion, or report a privacy concern.
