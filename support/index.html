Basics

What is INF™?

INF™ is an intranasal neuropeptide formula containing Semax, Selank, and Oxytocin. It was designed around the idea that if you can change how you think, then you can change how you are. INF™ exists to help support learning, clarity, calmness, and connection.

How does INF™ work?

Neuroplasticity is your brain's ability to change. Like a muscle, the thoughts, emotions, and actions you repeatedly practice strengthen over time. INF™ was designed to support a mental state that may be more conducive to learning, adaptation, and growth.

What does INF™ feel like?

Everyone's experience is different. Most people do not describe INF™ as stimulating. Instead, users often report feeling calmer, clearer, more focused, and more present. Some notice effects immediately, while others experience more subtle changes over time.

Who is INF™ for?

INF™ is intended for adults who want to learn faster, think more clearly, stay calmer under pressure, and connect more deeply with the people around them.

Science & Ingredients

Why Semax?

Semax was developed by Russian scientists studying learning, memory, attention, and recovery after brain injury. It was selected for INF™ because of its potential role in supporting neuroplasticity, cognitive performance, and the brain's ability to adapt to new information and experiences.

Why Selank?

Learning does not happen in a vacuum. Stress, anxiety, and mental tension can interfere with attention and decision-making. Selank was selected because it has been studied for its effects on calmness, emotional regulation, and mental clarity without the sedative effects commonly associated with traditional anti-anxiety medications.

Why Oxytocin?

Oxytocin is a naturally occurring neuropeptide involved in trust, bonding, empathy, and social connection. It was selected because meaningful growth rarely happens alone. Relationships, communication, and connection are often essential parts of becoming a better version of yourself.

Why combine all three?

Each ingredient was selected for a different reason. Semax for learning and adaptation. Selank for calmness and clarity. Oxytocin for connection and empathy. Together, they were chosen to support a mental state where growth, learning, and human connection can flourish.

Safety & Prescription

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. INF™ is available only after review and approval by a licensed healthcare provider. Not all patients will qualify.

Is INF™ a stimulant?

No. INF™ does not contain caffeine, amphetamines, or traditional stimulants.

Will INF™ make me dependent on it?

INF™ was designed around compounds studied for learning, emotional regulation, and social connection rather than stimulation or sedation. Individual experiences vary, and you should discuss any concerns with your healthcare provider.

What are the potential side effects?

As with any medication, side effects are possible. Potential side effects may include nasal irritation, headache, dizziness, or other effects depending on the individual. Your healthcare provider will review potential risks and benefits before prescribing treatment.
